Questions

Before you
get in touch

Something else? Ask us directly

Does an enquiry create a dealership or exclusivity?

No. An enquiry is the start of a conversation. A dealership, authorisation or exclusivity exists only once terms are agreed, approved by the company and signed.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, where appropriate. Product, company and commercial information can be exchanged under a non-disclosure agreement at the information-exchange stage.

Which categories are you looking for?

Business communications, CNG equipment, elevators and vertical transport, and solar energy. Equipment outside these areas is unlikely to be a fit for now.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We read every enquiry against our supply areas. Where there is a fit, we contact you to arrange an introduction call, which is stage one.
