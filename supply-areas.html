Something else? Ask us directly

Can you help specify the equipment?

Yes. We review your requirement and coordinate technical sizing or specification with qualified partners. The final specification is confirmed before any quotation is issued.

What warranty applies?

Manufacturer warranty terms vary by product. The applicable warranty and after-sales support are stated in each quotation.

Which standards and approvals apply?

Imported equipment and its installation must meet applicable Nigerian requirements. We confirm the relevant approvals for each product and project.
