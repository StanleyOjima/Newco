How an enquiry moves

Your use case, site, quantity and timeline. We start from what you need, not from a stock list.

Mission

To connect Nigerian businesses, institutions and projects with equipment that fits the specification, through supply routes that are verified, documented and supported.

  1. 01

    Verified

    Specifications and approvals are checked against supplier channels before anything is proposed.

  2. 02

    Documented

    Scope, terms and every party’s responsibility are set out in writing.

  3. 03

    Supported

    Installation and after-sales sit with qualified partners, named before you commit.

Operating approach

Three disciplines.
One line of responsibility.

We are a supply-led company. We do not present ourselves as a manufacturer or an installer. We bring the right parties together and make each one’s responsibility explicit.

Solution scoping

Understanding the requirement and turning it into a specification a supplier can quote against.

Sourcing & supply

Identifying supplier channels, checking documentation and coordinating delivery to site.

Partner delivery

Working with qualified technical partners for installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

What you can rely on

What we commit to, we put in writing.

  • Stock confirmed per quote

    Each supply area is open for enquiry. Stock and supplier appointments are confirmed in your quotation.

  • Terms in writing

    Availability, pricing and lead times are set out in every quotation.

  • Confirmed per project

    Approvals, warranty and installation responsibility are confirmed per product and project.

  • Straight answers

    If something is not yet verified, we tell you, and when it will be.
