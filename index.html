Questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else? Ask us directly

Do you sell from stock?

No. We source against your specification through supplier channels rather than selling from a stock list, so every quote reflects what your project actually needs.

Are you an authorised or exclusive dealer?

We only describe a supplier relationship once it has been formally agreed. Until then, we tell you where equipment comes from and on what terms.

Who installs and maintains the equipment?

Installation, commissioning and maintenance are carried out by qualified parties named in the scope, with each responsibility agreed in writing before any commitment.

Can I enquire with only a rough brief?

Yes. Send what you have. We ask for anything missing before verification starts, and nothing is committed until step four.

Can you supply push-to-talk and two-way radios?

Yes. Business communications is our main supply area. Tell us how many users, which sites and what the devices are for, and we confirm approvals, network and service terms before any offer.

Have a specification?
Start there.

Share what you need, where it is going and when. We’ll tell you plainly whether a suitable supply route exists.
